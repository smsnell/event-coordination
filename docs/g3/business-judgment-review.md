## 4. Practical usefulness for the Event and Operations Manager

The plan puts the recommended spend, capacity trade-off, category overruns, decision deadline and owner actions near the top. It retains twelve distinct 10-minute demos and twelve separate 3-minute changeovers. Inspection of the plan, baseline intervals and ICS shows sequential pairs at 11:15–12:33 and 13:33–14:51: 156 minutes total, with no demo/changeover overlap or duplicate demo. The keynote remains 10:00–11:00, with the first break at 11:00–11:15; closing ends at 17:00. These are workable exercise allocations, not observed delivery or proof of sufficient real staffing.

Arrival arithmetic is correctly labeled as a scenario: 180 arrivals in 15 minutes require 12 per minute; all 245 planning people require about 16.3 per minute. Neither rate is presented as measured desk capacity, and staff/arrival/access-assistance information is explicitly unknown. Operations should resolve that open question before publishing arrival instructions. The calendar and roster do not establish a desk count or assume that the 80% event attendance target must be achieved within the arrival window.

**BJ-03 — Medium: the draft calendar omits the sourced pre-event work.** [The ICS](../../deliverables/event-calendar.ics) contains the event-day setup, programme and teardown but no CAL-005 decision milestone, CAL-006 accessibility walkthrough or CAL-007 rehearsal. These are present in [the retained calendar source](../../deliverables/snapshots/evidence/calendar.json), snapshot 04 and the plan's calendar-source table: September 4 at 17:00, September 25 at 14:00–16:00, and October 16 at 15:00–18:00, respectively, all +08:00. Consequence: a manager using the supplied calendar can see the complete event day while missing the decision deadline and essential preparation. This is a practical completeness defect, not a claim that those records disappeared from the plan.

Recommendation: include tentative draft entries for those three sourced milestones, preserving owners, the venue-confirmation prerequisite for the walkthrough, and the optional/remote-backup meaning of rehearsal. A zero-duration decision milestone should not invent a meeting duration. Preserve the simulated dates and draft status; do not publish to a production calendar or imply that historical dates at the actual execution clock were completed. Carry equivalent entries into the conditional example with its unresolved conditions intact.
