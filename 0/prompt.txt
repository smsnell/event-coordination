## 2. Produce DESIGN-PLAN.md

Aim for roughly 1,000–1,500 words plus compact tables; reference
requirements rather than copying them. Exceed that only to explain a
material requirement or unresolved decision.

Include:

- Business outcome, minimum scope, explicit exclusions, and material
  unknowns.
- The simplest architecture and only the significant alternatives
  and trade-offs.
- AI versus deterministic responsibilities, prompting versus training,
  current-source retrieval versus model knowledge, permitted tools,
  and human review.
- Minimal records/interfaces for identity, states, provenance,
  decisions, and approval handling.
- How the nine required snapshots are produced at their actual
  boundaries, with same-run predecessor hashes and consumed/produced
  references.
- How one shared planning basis produces four consistent drafts,
  including materially different options, rejected-option reasons,
  integer TWD amounts, and an actual non-overlapping programme with
  dependencies—not just a total duration.
- Official floor-plan/image handling, page/region evidence, and limits
  on spatial/accessibility conclusions.
- Correct complete/partial/blocked/failed behavior, history
  preservation, changed inputs, and recovery.
- One feasible end-to-end invocation, prerequisites, source access,
  and minimal dependencies. Identify whether it needs an invoking
  agent or another model connection; do not assume unverified host
  capabilities or introduce a second model service by default.
- A compact mapping of grouped requirements to implementation phases
  and acceptance checks. Cover every mandatory behavior without
  creating a separate leaf-by-leaf bureaucracy.

Use a few coherent phases. The nine runtime snapshots are required
artifacts, not nine mandatory agent sessions, development gates, or
separate subsystems.

Avoid generic frameworks, speculative hardening, duplicated validators,
exhaustive fault campaigns, and unnecessary parallelism.

Recommend a starting model/effort and escalation condition per phase
using choices actually known to be available. Recommendations do not
authorize changing models or launching workers in this planning task.

specified partial/blocked/failure behavior, not invented success.

G2 — Change and recovery

combined row/header reordering plus unrelated extra columns, invalid
required data, preserved history, propagation to all four drafts, and
repair of missing/corrupt output.

Preserve approval and unsent-message boundaries.

G3 — Final acceptance and handover

Inspect the actual final artifacts and current run bindings; run
necessary regression/integration and security checks; perform one
independent review and an operator walkthrough when available.

Distinguish independent technical verification from actual human use.
Missing human feedback remains explicit, not simulated.

Where AI-generated judgment needs evaluation, propose one bounded
second-model review with explicit criteria for evidence grounding,
trade-offs, uncertainty, and usefulness—not another general code audit.

For each gate, specify objective acceptance evidence, what I should
personally inspect, unresolved limitations, and a commit checkpoint
after my acceptance.

Do not require a separate full diff audit for every small component.
After corrections, rerun affected checks; expand only when dependencies
or risk justify it.
